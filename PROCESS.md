# PROCESS.md — AI use for Assignment 1

**Tools used**: I used DeepSeek. I asked it to help me analyse the assignment brief, translate my Chinese brainstorming into English, and generate a first draft based on my structured outline. I then revised and polished the content myself.

**What I kept**: I kept the core structure of the essay—starting with the reading versus writing question, moving into my personal internship experience, and then discussing the qualities needed. The AI helped me articulate the flow and suggested useful transitions.

**What I rejected**: The AI initially suggested a generic paragraph about "machine learning is the future." I rejected it because it was too broad and generic. I replaced it with concrete examples from my own experience in internet operations—like scraping trending keywords and sorting videos by play count. I also rejected the AI's default assumption that I needed to become a "programmer." I am not becoming a programmer; I am becoming a better operator who understands code.

**My process**:
- Brainstormed ideas with DeepSeek (25 minutes)
- Outlined 5 main sections (20 minutes)
- Wrote a draft by expanding each section (50 minutes)
- Edited for clarity and word count (35 minutes)
- Verified all citations against actual sources (40 minutes)

I did not copy-paste entire paragraphs without rewriting; all final text is my own, shaped by the AI's suggestions but adjusted to reflect my own perspective.
